Welcome to the official digital platform repository for **Repi Kalehiwet Bible College (ረጲ ቃለ ሕይወት መጽሐፍ ቅዱስ ኮሌጅ)**—an institution dedicated to grounding believers in Scripture, fostering godly character, and equipping dynamic Christian leaders for ministry in Ethiopia and beyond.

---

## 🌟 About the Institution

Located in Addis Ababa within the Repi Kalehiwet Church compound (under the Southwest Synod/Union of the Ethiopian Kale Heywet Church), the college was established in **2015 E.C.** to meet the growing need for deep scriptural grounding in the 21st century. 

This platform serves as a modern institutional gateway, showcasing academic programs, core values, institutional mission & vision, interactive photo streams, and student admission inquiries.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Experience

* **🏛️ Institutional Overview & Heritage:** Comprehensive details on the college's founding history, background within the Kale Heywet Church community, and spiritual objectives.
* **📜 Academic Curriculum:** Structured overviews of Diploma and Degree programs focusing on Biblical Studies, Ministry Foundations, and Christian Leadership.
* **💎 Foundational Pillars (Core Values):** 
  1. **Authority:** Accepting the Bible as the final authority.
  2. **Character:** Cultivating the character of Christ.
  3. **Guidance:** Being led by the Holy Spirit.
  4. **Discipleship:** Raising trained disciples sent out for ministry.
* **🎯 Mission & Vision Statements:** Dedicated panels highlighting the college's calling to raise biblically sound, Spirit-led leaders.
* **📸 Infinite Marquee & Photo Carousel:** Dynamic dual-row continuous scrolling photo streams and interactive animated image galleries for campus archives.
* **✉️ Admissions & Contact Portal:** Integrated inquiry form for prospective students and church partners.
* **🌓 Modern Glassmorphism UI:** Built with Tailwind CSS, custom dark-themed aesthetics, glowing accents, responsive mobile navigation, and AOS (Animate on Scroll) effects.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Markup & Styling:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS (via CDN Engine), Custom CSS Variables & Glassmorphism effects.
* **Interactivity & Motion:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), AOS (Animate on Scroll Library), Font Awesome 6.4.0 Icon Systems.
* **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Poppins` & `Noto Sans Ethiopic` for seamless bilingual English-Amharic rendering).
